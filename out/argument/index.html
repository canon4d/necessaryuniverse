Existence of a Time-Orientable Lorentzian Metric

Thm. 4.1

The Klein Block K\KleinK admits a smooth Lorentzian metric ggg of signature (+,−,−,−)(+,-,-,-)(+,−,−,−) such that the spatial S3\SthreeS3 fibers are everywhere spacelike and the spacetime is time-orientable.

Closed Timelike Orbits for Transverse Fields

Thm. 4.6

Let (K,g)(\Klein,g)(K,g) be equipped with any time-orientable Lorentzian metric such that the spatial S3\SthreeS3 fibers are spacelike. Then every smooth future-directed timelike vector field TTT on K\KleinK has at least one closed orbit.

No Global Real-Valued Time Function

Cor. 4.7

No smooth function t:K→Rt:\Klein\to\Rt:K→R can be strictly increasing along every future-directed timelike curve. In particular, K\KleinK admits no global time function in the usual causality-theoretic sense.
